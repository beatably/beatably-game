## Pre-publication checks

1. Confirm which Beatably-owned accounts exist and that the user is signed in.
2. Confirm the final video contains only audio and imagery Beatably is allowed to publish on each platform.
3. Deploy and verify campaign-to-game-session attribution before using the UTM links.
4. Put the platform-specific tracked link in the corresponding profile.
5. Preview the first frame, safe-zone placement, captions, cover, and audio before publishing.
6. Ask for approval immediately before the first public post on each account.

## Measurement decision

Evaluate each platform independently after seven days.

- Continue: at least 1,000 views or 5% engagement, and at least 3 tracked game starts.
- Iterate hook: watch retention is weak but profile visits or comments show interest.
- Iterate profile/CTA: views and engagement are healthy but tracked visits or starts are weak.
- Pause platform: two materially different videos both fail to produce meaningful engagement or tracked starts.

The second test changes only the opening hook to `Think you know music better than your friends?` so the result can be compared with the first video.
