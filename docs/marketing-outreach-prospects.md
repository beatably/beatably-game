# Beatably outreach shortlist

Prepared 9 August 2026. No messages have been sent. Every contact below requires a fresh relevance check and approval immediately before outreach.

## Outreach method

- Start with 10 Swedish quiz hosts/venues, because one successful trial can put Beatably in front of several real groups at once.
- Follow with 6 English-language discovery and creator targets.
- Send individually, not as a bulk campaign. Mention one specific detail from the target's current work.
- Give every target a unique `utm_content` value so their traffic and game starts can be measured independently.
- Initial message should ask for a playtest or a small trial, not demand coverage.
- Follow up at most once after five days.

Tracked link pattern:

`https://beatably.app/?utm_source=creator_outreach&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=organic_launch_2026&utm_content=PROSPECT_ID`

## Send order and decision rules

1. Batch 1 after approval: prospects 1–5, one message at a time.
2. Wait 48 hours and inspect replies plus tagged game starts.
3. If at least one positive reply or two attributed game starts occur, send Swedish prospects 6–10.
4. English batch begins with Gamefolio, Indie Game Buzz, and CGDannyB. IndieGameInn is deferred until a copyright-cleared 5–10 minute commentary video exists; its current rules do not accept a short trailer alone.
5. Stop or rewrite if the first 10 messages produce zero replies and zero game starts. Do not solve low relevance by increasing volume.
