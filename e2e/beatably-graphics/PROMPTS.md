# Image-generation prompt set

Built-in image generation was used with the supplied Beatably mascot artwork as the identity reference.

## Landscape master

Premium 16:9 stylized 3D game key art: exact mint-turquoise Beatably mascot with relaxed half-lidded eyes, small smile and purple-magenta headphones; deep midnight cosmic studio; cyan/magenta nebula; abstract blank timeline cards; mascot on the right with clean left-side negative space; no text, numbers, logos, UI, album art or Apple branding.

## Square master

Premium 1:1 stylized 3D directory cover: exact Beatably mascot centered slightly low; abstract blank timeline cards in a glowing ring; clean dark top band for the authentic logo; no generated text or branding.

## Portrait master

Premium 4:5 stylized 3D press/social key art: exact Beatably mascot in the lower-right; abstract blank timeline cards rising diagonally; clean dark upper-left area; no generated text or branding.

## Expression edits

- **Wink:** one eye gently winking, other eye half-lidded, slightly wider asymmetric closed-mouth grin.
- **Excited:** both eyes open and a small joyful open smile, suggesting a correct placement.
- **Curious:** half-lidded side-eye looking up-left with a small thoughtful sideways smile.

For all expression edits, the composition, lighting, body, materials, headphones, background and crop were locked; only eyes, eyelids and mouth were changed.

The supplied transparent `beatably_logo.png` was composited afterward so the wordmark remains exact rather than AI-generated.
